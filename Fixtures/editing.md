## Typing

1. Put the caret at the end of this line and press Return: the list continues.
   - A nested item: Tab indents, ⇧Tab outdents.
2. Return on an empty item ends the list.

> Return continues quotes too.

Type `(`, `[`, `{`, `"` or a backtick: the closer appears, typing it skips over it, and Backspace removes the pair.

Select a word and press ⌘B, ⌘I, ⌘E or ⌘K. Select two lines and press ⇧⌘8, ⇧⌘7, ⇧⌘9 or ⇧⌘.
Put the caret on a line and press ⌘1 to ⌘6 for headings. ⌘Z undoes each of them in one step.
